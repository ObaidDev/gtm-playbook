# The Campaign is the unit of demand work

Demand work is organised around Campaigns: one hypothesis about the ICP, tested with exactly one Lead magnet and the Artifacts that promote it, ending in a Verdict. Each Campaign gets its own folder in the founder's Workspace holding its definition (hypothesis, target signal, judge-by date) and its Artifacts; Experiment log entries are trials inside a Campaign, and Customer conversations may link to one. We chose per-Campaign folders over a flat, type-grouped Artifact store because the retro judges one hypothesis at a time and needs everything about it in one place, and because a Lead magnet without a hypothesis is the "post and hope" pattern the sources warn against. Lifecycle transitions each have one owning skill: `plan-campaign` creates (planned), `log-experiment` makes it live on the first entry, `gtm-retro` judges.
