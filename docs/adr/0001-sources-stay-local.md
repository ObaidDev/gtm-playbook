# Sources stay local; skills paraphrase and credit

Sources (video transcripts and similar material) are third-party content, often padded with promotion, and publishing them verbatim raises copyright questions that cannot be undone once pushed. So `sources/` is gitignored and never published: skills distill the ideas in their own words, drop all promotional content, and end with a one-line credit naming the creator and the Source title. New Sources enter through `/ingest-source`, which produces distilled notes rather than copies.
