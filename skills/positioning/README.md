# Positioning

Skills that define who the product is for and what it claims, each writing one section of your Profile.

## User-invoked

Reachable only when you type them (`disable-model-invocation: true`).

- **[define-icp](./define-icp/SKILL.md)**: Define your ICP (who has the urgent and important problem your product solves, and its early adopters), the one social platform they spend time on, and the Lead stage question that tells an ICP lead apart. Run after `setup-gtm`, and again when Customer conversations say your ICP is wrong.
- **[value-prop](./value-prop/SKILL.md)**: Write your one-sentence Value proposition: how your product is ten times better than the alternatives at solving your ICP's problem, challenging any claim that is not. Run after `define-icp`, and again when the ICP changes or Customer conversations say the claim is not landing.
- **[outcome-positioning](./outcome-positioning/SKILL.md)**: Sell growth or risk reduction instead of productivity, and decide what to show instead of workflows and features: the work getting done, the context and harness that beat a general model, and your integrations (including MCP and human handoffs). Run after `value-prop`, and again when the Value proposition changes or Customer conversations ask what the ROI is.
- **[outcome-pricing](./outcome-pricing/SKILL.md)**: Price on outcomes instead of seats: a platform fee with unlimited seats and included usage, plus an overage for each unit of outcome beyond it, challenging any per-seat price. Run after `outcome-positioning`, and again when the Outcome positioning changes or Customer conversations push back on price.
