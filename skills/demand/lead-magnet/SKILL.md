# Lead magnet

Outline the one **Lead magnet** of a planned Campaign and save it as `gtm/campaigns/<slug>/lead-magnet.md`. Read [STATE.md](../../meta/setup-gtm/STATE.md) first: it gives the format and the rules for the `gtm/` folder. Never write the Profile, `campaign.md`, or any other Artifact.

## What makes a good Lead magnet

- **A checklist or guide** that **solves one specific problem** of the ICP.
- **A distinct point of view** on that problem, not the advice the ICP already hears everywhere.
- **Educational.** It teaches; it does not pitch the product.

Posts link to it on a landing page, never "comment below": buyers with budget lurk and do not comment, but they take what solves their problem. Taking it shows they have that problem, and warm outbound opens from it.

## Process

1. **Check.** Read `gtm/profile.md` and follow the missing-prerequisite rule in STATE.md. Stop without writing or interviewing if the Profile is missing (`/gtm-playbook:setup-gtm`), the ICP is a placeholder (`/gtm-playbook:define-icp`), the Value proposition is a placeholder (`/gtm-playbook:value-prop`), or no Campaign is `planned` without a `lead-magnet.md` (`/gtm-playbook:plan-campaign`). A Campaign has exactly one Lead magnet: to test another, plan a new Campaign. If several Campaigns qualify and the Founder named none, ask which.

2. **Explore.** Finding facts is your job. Take Form, Problem, and Point of view from the Campaign's Lead magnet line and the slice of the ICP from its Hypothesis; do not decide them again. Read the whole Profile, `gtm/conversations/` for the ICP's own words, and anything else on the problem. Note every `(unconfirmed)` field you build on.

3. **Interview.** Call the Skill tool with `gtm-playbook:interview`, on the Outline: every item of the checklist or section of the guide, held to the tests above. A different problem or point of view is a different hypothesis: send the Founder to `/gtm-playbook:plan-campaign`. If the Founder says to write now, the Outline can never be left open.

4. **Confirm, then write.** Title it after the one problem it solves. Show the draft in a message that asks nothing else, and write only once the Founder confirms it (or already told you to write without confirming). If they reject the title, record it as an Open question. Check again that the Campaign is still `planned` with no `lead-magnet.md`, then write it in the STATE.md format.

5. **Done.** Say where the file is, that the Founder writes the Lead magnet out from the outline before the Campaign's publish-by date, and that the other Artifacts on its **Artifacts needed** list come next. Name any `(unconfirmed)` field it builds on and the skill that settles it.
